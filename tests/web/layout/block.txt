# Block layout (CSS 2.1 chapters 8-10): widths, margins and their collapsing, floats, positioning.
# Every case is a page laid out in a viewport of 800x600 (unless [w=...]) with the built-in fixed-width font: at 16px each
# character is 8px wide, line-height normal is 16px with the baseline 12px below the top. After "---" come lines the
# layout must print (tests/host/webtool.c "layout"): "#id X Y W H" border boxes in page coordinates, "#id lines N",
# "#id line K Y H BASELINE", '#id frag "TEXT" X Y W'. The values were worked out by hand from the specification.

=== width, auto margins, padding and border
<style>body{margin:0} #a{width:200px;margin:0 auto;height:10px} #b{width:50%;margin-left:auto;height:10px}
#c{width:100px;padding:10px;border:5px solid;height:20px}</style>
<div id=a></div><div id=b></div><div id=c></div>
---
#a 300 0 200 10
#b 400 10 400 10
#c 0 20 130 50

=== adjoining sibling margins collapse, negative ones add
<style>body{margin:0} div{height:10px} #a{margin-bottom:20px} #b{margin-top:30px;margin-bottom:-5px} #c{margin-top:10px}</style>
<div id=a></div><div id=b></div><div id=c></div>
---

#b 0 40 800 10
#c 0 55 800 10

=== a parent's margins collapse with its first and last child's
<style>body{margin:0} #p{margin:10px 0} #c{margin:20px 0;height:10px} #n{height:10px}</style>
<div id=p><div id=c></div></div><div id=n></div>
---
#p 0 20 800 10
#c 0 20 800 10
#n 0 50 800 10

=== padding separates the parent's margin from the child's
<style>body{margin:0} #p{margin:10px 0;padding-top:1px} #c{margin:20px 0;height:10px}</style>
<div id=p><div id=c></div></div>
---
#p 0 10 800 31
#c 0 31 800 10

=== an empty block lets the margins collapse through it
