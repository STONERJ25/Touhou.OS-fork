A mansion appears beyond the bamboo

Reports reached this paper of a red mansion standing where the lake had been empty the day before.

The reporter set out before the sun rose, camera in hand, and followed the shore until the fog thinned. What stood there was not a dream: walls the colour of late autumn leaves, a clock tower, and a gate that nobody answered. A maid in the garden declined to give her name, though she did offer tea, which was excellent.

The mistress of the house is not receiving visitors at this hour.

Further investigation is planned for the next full moon. Readers with photographs are asked to send them in.
